# CampusMind - B.Tech CSE Admission Brochure 2026

## 1. Overview & Eligibility
Welcome to CampusMind College of Engineering and Technology.
The B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) program offers state-of-the-art education in Software Engineering, AI/ML, Cybersecurity, and Cloud Computing.

### Eligibility Criteria:
- Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board.
- Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).
- Candidates must possess a valid JEE Main or State Entrance Exam score.

## 2. Fee Structure & Payment
- Annual Tuition Fee: $4,500 per academic year.
- Development Fee: $500 per academic year.
- Refund Policy: 100% tuition refund if withdrawn 15 days before the academic session commences. 50% refund within the first 30 days.

## 3. Scholarships & Financial Aid
- Merit Scholarship: 100% tuition fee waiver for students scoring in the top 1% of JEE Main.
- Need-based Aid: Available for family annual income under $10,000 upon submitting income proof.
