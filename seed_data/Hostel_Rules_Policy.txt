# CampusMind - Hostel & Campus Conduct Policy

## 1. Hostel Accommodation Rules
- CampusMind provides separate hostel facilities for male and female students.
- All first-year outstation students are mandatory residents unless given special exemption by the Dean of Student Affairs.

### Hostel Timings & Curfew:
- Hostel gates close strictly at 10:00 PM on weekdays (Monday to Friday).
- Weekend curfew is 10:30 PM (Saturday and Sunday).
- Late pass permissions must be requested via the Hostel Warden Portal at least 6 hours in advance.

## 2. Facilities & Amenities
- Wi-Fi Access: High-speed internet is available 24/7 across all hostel blocks.
- Mess Services: Breakfast (7:30 AM - 9:30 AM), Lunch (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM), Snacks (5:00 PM - 6:00 PM), Dinner (7:30 PM - 9:30 PM).

## 3. Code of Conduct & Anti-Ragging
- Zero-tolerance policy for ragging, harassment, or substance abuse.
- Violations result in immediate suspension and police escalation as per university regulations.
